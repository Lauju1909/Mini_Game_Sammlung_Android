Audio Mini Games is a rich collection of accessible audio-based mini games playable entirely through sound, spatial cues, and haptics without requiring visual feedback.

Features:
- Over 60 mini-games across Action & Reaction, Logic & Memory, 3D Stereo Navigation, Trivia, and Simulation
- 100% playable with TalkBack or built-in speech engine
- 3D spatial stereo sound design
- Haptic feedback support
- Local high scores and achievement tracking
- 100% free, no ads, no trackers, fully playable offline
